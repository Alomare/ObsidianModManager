### Changes

- Update checks moved to a column between On and Mod, with a button for each mod, each folder and the whole list.
- Mod versions have their own column again, before Scope.
- The Scope column shows only the diamond. Hover over it for the name.
- Mod descriptions left the list and show when you hover over a mod.
- Bigger mod images and smaller switches in the list.
- Remove in a mod's details is now a bin under the close button.
- Turn On and Turn Off in the bar for checked mods are icons.
- Create Folder is blue, with a folder outline and a plus inside.
- Purge, Deploy and Play send a wave of color across the top bar.
- The installer shows the Obsidian logo, and the app icon fills the taskbar.
