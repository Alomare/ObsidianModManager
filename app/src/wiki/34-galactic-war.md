## Modded missions don't count

Every player shares one Galactic War. A session mod that makes missions easier, or harder, would push the whole war in one direction for everyone else.

While any session content is deployed, the loader stops your game from reporting mission results and statistics to the shared war. Your own save still updates: you keep the XP, unlocks and medals you earn.

Client mods don't change this. With only client mods, your missions count as normal.

> [!NOTE]
> The block applies as long as session content is deployed, even in a mission where your session mods are switched off. Purge or deploy without session mods to take part in the war again.

## The war on Home

The panel at the top right of Home shows the war calendar, a small map and how many Helldivers are fighting. On the map, Super Earth sits in the middle with a cone for each enemy; the light part of a cone is the ground already liberated. Hover a cone to see the region being fought over, or Super Earth to see whether it is under attack. Obsidian reads the war from the official HELLDIVERS server every five minutes while Home is open.

The button in the corner of the panel folds it away.

For the history of past seasons and detailed statistics, see [helldivers.bot](https://helldivers.bot), a fan site with no ties to Arrowhead or to Obsidian.
